# Dynamic Asset Builder surface brief

## Scope and visitor mode

- **Surface:** external native asset-authoring and review tool, not player UI.
- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Primary job:** edit a named semantic shape recipe and immediately inspect the deterministic mesh candidate it produces.
- **Audience:** an asset author or reviewer working from the measured design-language corpus.
- **Constraints:** use the real Rust Dynamic Asset Generator; no voxel scaling, raw hash editing, detached mock state, or claim that an unpromoted candidate is already a runtime game asset.

## Direction contract

### THESIS

The mesh is the evidence. Each recipe change must visibly transform one coherent asset, while the derived hash records the exact candidate and the source corpus remains inspectable.

### OWN-WORLD

A restrained industrial/optical instrument in the established game vocabulary: matte blue-black surfaces, precise pale measurements, one warm construction accent, and compact technical labels. Orb handles are tactile slider knobs, not glowing 3D gizmos.

### STORY

The author sees a settings gear as the first reusable corpus proof. Three named controls—Teeth, Opening, and Accent—edit continuous recipe axes. Teeth morph the profile, Opening changes the bore relative to the root radius, and Accent moves continuously through authored construction states. Every edit rebuilds one combined mesh and derives a new candidate hash.

### FIRST VIEWPORT

A narrow control rail occupies the left side; the large right stage presents the gear from a slight three-quarter view. Each slider has a visible orb handle, exact value, named corpus meaning, and keyboard equivalent. The derived builder hash and review state sit at the top of the rail. The gear remains the dominant object at first glance.

### FORM

Code-led native Operate surface extending the established renderer and UI tokens. Three conventional horizontal sliders, one preview stage, direct camera orbit, and no modal or nested-card structure.

### FINISH

unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved work

- Corpus endpoint calibration and admissible source licences remain review work under CAP-010.
- OpenPBR authoring/validation, manifests, reference browser, and human promotion remain open.
- Family-specific recipes beyond the gear are future work; the schema must not hard-code the gear as the product.
